## Summary

**Recommended Interview Preparation Strategy**:

1. **Weeks 1-2**: Review data structures and algorithms fundamentals
2. **Weeks 3-6**: Practice coding problems on LeetCode (easy and medium)
3. **Weeks 7-8**: Study system design with Grokking course
4. **Weeks 9-10**: Practice mock interviews on Pramp or Interviewing.io
5. **Weeks 11-12**: Review Java-specific topics and behavioral questions

**Key Takeaways**:
- LeetCode is essential for coding practice
- Grokking System Design is best for system design
- Cracking the Coding Interview is the interview bible
- Mock interviews are crucial for preparation
- Practice consistently and review solutions
- Understand concepts, not just memorize
- Communicate clearly during interviews
- Manage time effectively

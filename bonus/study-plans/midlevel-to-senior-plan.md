# Mid-Level to Senior Progression Plan

## Overview

A structured 10-week plan to transition from mid-level engineer to senior engineer. This plan focuses on developing architectural expertise, system design mastery, leadership capabilities, and strategic thinking. It emphasizes ownership, impact, and mentoring.

**Who this is for:**
- Mid-level developers (3-5 years experience) wanting to advance
- Those preparing for senior-level interviews
- Professionals wanting to take on architectural responsibilities
- People ready to mentor and lead teams

**What it covers:**
- Advanced system design and architecture
- Distributed systems and microservices
- Performance optimization and scalability
- Database design and optimization
- Leadership and mentoring
- Strategic thinking and decision-making
- Code review and quality standards
- Technical communication and documentation

## Time Commitment

- **Daily**: 2-3 hours
- **Total**: 140-210 hours over 70 days
- **Schedule**: 6 days a week (1 day rest)
- **Recommended**: Morning (1.5 hours) + Evening (1 hour)

## Prerequisites

- 3-5 years of Java development experience
- Strong understanding of system design
- Experience with microservices architecture
- Leadership experience (mentoring, code review)
- Ability to design and implement complex systems

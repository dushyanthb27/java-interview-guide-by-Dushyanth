# Senior to Staff Progression Plan

## Overview

A structured 12-week plan to transition from senior engineer to staff/principal engineer. This plan focuses on developing strategic vision, organizational impact, and thought leadership. It emphasizes influence across teams, architectural decisions, and long-term strategy.

**Who this is for:**
- Senior engineers (5+ years experience) wanting to advance
- Those preparing for staff/principal-level interviews
- Professionals wanting to increase organizational impact
- People ready to influence strategy and architecture

**What it covers:**
- Organizational strategy and vision
- Large-scale system architecture
- Technical decision-making frameworks
- Organizational influence and leadership
- Thought leadership and communication
- Mentoring and team development
- Cross-functional collaboration
- Long-term technical roadmapping

## Time Commitment

- **Daily**: 2-3 hours
- **Total**: 168-252 hours over 84 days
- **Schedule**: 6 days a week (1 day rest)
- **Recommended**: Morning (1.5 hours) + Evening (1 hour)

## Prerequisites

- 5+ years of Java development experience
- Strong system design and architecture expertise
- Leadership experience (mentoring, team management)
- Experience with large-scale systems
- Strategic thinking and decision-making experience

## Week 11-12: Mock Interviews & Final Preparation

### Week 11: Mock Interviews - Staff Level
**Focus**: Full interview simulation for staff level
**Time**: 10-12 hours total

**Daily Schedule**:
- Morning (1 hour): Preparation
  - Review: Key concepts and frameworks
  - Prepare: Design tools and materials
  - Warm-up: Review 2 complex problems

- Mid-morning (2.5 hours): Staff-Level Interview
  - Simulate: Full interview (120 minutes)
  - Problem: Complex organizational/architectural problem
  - Evaluation: Vision, strategy, communication

- Afternoon (1 hour): Feedback and analysis
  - Review: Recording of interview
  - Identify: Areas for improvement
  - Plan: Focused practice for weak areas

- Evening (1 hour): Documentation
  - Document: Interview performance
  - Note: Weak areas and improvement areas

**Objectives**:
- [ ] Complete full staff-level interview
- [ ] Identify weak areas
- [ ] Plan improvement strategy
- [ ] Build confidence

**Practice Problems**:
- Solve complex organizational problem
- Answer follow-up questions
- Discuss strategy and vision

**Review Checklist**:
- [ ] Completed interview
- [ ] Recorded and reviewed performance
- [ ] Identified improvement areas
- [ ] Ready for focused practice

You're ready for staff-level interviews when you can:

✓ **Strategic Vision**: Create and communicate technical vision for organization

✓ **Architecture**: Design systems at enterprise scale with proper trade-offs

✓ **Decision-Making**: Make and communicate complex technical decisions

✓ **Influence**: Influence across teams and organization effectively

✓ **Mentoring**: Mentor and develop senior engineers

✓ **Thought Leadership**: Communicate technical ideas with impact

✓ **Communication**: Explain complex ideas clearly to various audiences

✓ **Problem-Solving**: Solve complex organizational problems

✓ **Mock Interviews**: Pass staff-level interviews with 90%+ performance

✓ **Behavioral**: Answer behavioral and leadership questions confidently

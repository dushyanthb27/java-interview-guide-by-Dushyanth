## Overview
Technical interviews increasingly include behavioral questions to assess soft skills, leadership, and problem-solving approach. These scenarios help prepare for the non-coding aspects of senior+ interviews.

## Prerequisites
- Professional software development experience
- Understanding of software development lifecycle

## Key Takeaways
- Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for structured answers
- Demonstrate both technical depth and communication skills
- Show ownership, accountability, and growth mindset
- Emphasize collaboration and team success over individual heroics
- Be honest about failures and what you learned from them
